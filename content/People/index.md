Every vote and quote on this site traces to a person. This page is organized two ways: by **where** the decision is made (the city, the county, the counties around us, the state and Washington, and the people doing the public's work), and within each, by **the next date voters can act on the record**. Titles and promises are revisable; election day is the one check [[Oversight Theater|officials can't revise]].

**The headline for November 3, 2026:** four of the six councilmembers who voted yes on the [[What Is the RTIC|RTIC]] are on the ballot, the only no vote is running for mayor, the sheriff who built the county's ~2,000-camera network is running for a third term, and five more WNC sheriffs' races decide who runs the cameras next door. Early voting starts **October 15**. The full ballot view: [[Election 2026]].
