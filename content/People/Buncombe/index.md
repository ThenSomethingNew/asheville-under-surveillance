The elected deciders (council, mayor, sheriff, and their challengers) are on the main [[People/index|People]] page. These are the officials and institutions behind them: the board that funds the county network, the staff who certify the money, the school boards that control the cameras, the housing authority whose residents live under them, the police command that runs the city's system, and the downtown bodies that recruited cameras for it.
