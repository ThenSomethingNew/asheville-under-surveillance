# Asheville Under Surveillance

The sourced record of how mass surveillance came to Asheville, North Carolina: every vote, quote, and dollar tied to primary documents and timestamped to the city's own meeting videos.

**Live site: https://thensomethingnew.github.io/asheville-under-surveillance/**

## What this is

On May 12, 2026, Asheville City Council voted 6 to 1 to accept $1.14 million in federal funding and authorize a 7.5-year contract with Axon for a Real-Time Intelligence Center, a police hub connecting license plate readers, body cameras, drones, and private security cameras. This site, written by an Asheville resident, documents how that decision was made, what led up to it, who decides what happens next, and how residents can get involved.

The site includes:

- A timeline of the surveillance buildout from 2022 to the present, with sources for every entry
- Detailed write-ups of the key city meetings, reconstructed from the city's own videos with timestamps
- Profiles of every official and candidate involved, organized around the November 2026 ballot
- Pre-written public records requests any person can file under North Carolina law
- Regional coverage of surveillance decisions across Western North Carolina
- Explainers on what the technology collects and what the national record shows about how it gets used

## How it's built

The site is a [Quartz v4](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) static site. Content lives in `content/` as Markdown. Pushes to `main` build and deploy to GitHub Pages via GitHub Actions.

## Corrections

Every page shows where its facts come from. If you find an error, open an issue with a link to the page and the source that contradicts it. Corrections are the point of publishing sources.
